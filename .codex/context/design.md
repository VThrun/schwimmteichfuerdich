# Design direction

Last verified: August 20, 2026

selected for this project and the existing Preline integration. It is separate
from Design Draft 2 and has no daisyUI Blueprint or Blueprint MCP dependency.

a redesign or migration. Treat imagery, prices, technical details, contact data,
and legal content as placeholders until approved.
