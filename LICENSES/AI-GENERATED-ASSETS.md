## Prompt set

All prompts requested a premium, photorealistic editorial image for a German
landscaping website, set in a Central European garden with a warm, natural palette
of deep green, ochre, timber and dark water. They explicitly excluded visible
faces, blue tiled pools, text, logos and watermarks.

Individual motifs:

1. A compact natural swimming pond with a timber deck in a small, lush garden.
2. A generous natural swimming pond with a broad timber terrace and planted banks.
3. A long, linear natural pool with dark water, timber construction and dense planting.
4. A cozy evening scene with only a person's lower legs and feet dangling over the pond.
5. Close-up hands planting water plants into a natural pond edge during DIY construction.
6. A living pond biotope with a dragonfly, frog and native aquatic plants.
7. Integrated garden landscaping with a swimming pond, timber terrace, natural-stone wall and paving.
